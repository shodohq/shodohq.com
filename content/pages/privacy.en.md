## 2. Information we collect

- Information you enter when you contact us, apply for a PoC, or report a vulnerability, such as your company, department and title, name, email address, and message
- Information entrusted to us under agreed terms during a PoC or design partnership
- Information about how you use our website, such as access logs and cookies

## 3. How we use it

- To answer your inquiries and contact you as needed
- To arrange and run PoCs and design partnerships
- To send you information about our products and services
- To respond to vulnerability reports
- To understand and improve how our website is used

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide personal data to third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

## 5. Service providers

We may entrust part of the handling of personal data to service providers within the scope of the purposes above. When we do, we select them carefully and supervise them appropriately.

## 7. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand and improve how our website is used. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as the pages you visit and how long you stay. This information does not include your name, email address, or other information that directly identifies you.

The information is handled in accordance with Google’s privacy policy. To learn how Google uses it, see “[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).”

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, or stop measurement with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
