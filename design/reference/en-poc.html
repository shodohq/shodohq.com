Who we would like
to hear from

Critical infrastructure operators

Operators preparing regulatory notifications or taking stock of their contractors.

Essential service providers

Organizations that want to build outage readiness into BCP reviews and tabletop exercises.

Manufacturers and logistics companies

Companies at the heart of supply chains that want to know in advance what happens if orders or shipments stop.

System integrators and consultancies

Firms that want to use Pixie in compliance or security support engagements.

How it works

  1. 1Contact usTell us which products interest you and what challenges you face.

  4. 4Run the PoCYou use the products in your real environment.
  5. 5ReviewWe go over the results together and discuss next steps.
