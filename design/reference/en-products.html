Pixie is a family of security products built to protect your business without bringing it to a halt. Four products map your attack surface, and Pixie for Operations uses that material to show what to isolate and what will keep running.

Pixie
for Operations

Decision AI for keeping operations running during an attack.

It builds an outage map from your inventories, network diagrams, contracts, and runbooks. When an attack hits, it proposes containment plans that stop the spread while keeping as much critical business running as possible, explained in terms executives can act on.

The Pixie series (beta)

Four products that map
your attack surface.

Each product can be deployed on its own. The asset and attack-surface data they collect becomes raw material for the outage map in Pixie for Operations. All four are in beta, and we are looking for companies to try them in a proof of concept.

CAASM Cyber Asset Attack Surface ManagementConsolidates scattered asset information into a single inventory. Beta
EASM External Attack Surface ManagementDiscovers externally visible domains and services to reveal your points of entry. Beta
IASM Internal Attack Surface ManagementMaps the attack surface inside your internal network. Beta
ASPM Application Security Posture ManagementManages application vulnerabilities and risk from development through operations. Beta
