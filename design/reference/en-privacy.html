2. Information we collect

  • Information you enter when you contact us, apply for a PoC, or report a vulnerability, such as your company, department and title, name, email address, and message
  • Information entrusted to us under agreed terms during a PoC or design partnership
  • Information about how you use our website, such as access logs and cookies

3. How we use it

  • To answer your inquiries and contact you as needed
  • To arrange and run PoCs and design partnerships
  • To send you information about our products and services
  • To respond to vulnerability reports
  • To understand and improve how our website is used

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide personal data to third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

5. Service providers

We may entrust part of the handling of personal data to service providers within the scope of the purposes above. When we do, we select them carefully and supervise them appropriately.

6. Security measures

Organizational
We appoint a person responsible for personal data and review how it is handled.
Personnel
We train our staff in handling personal data.
Physical
We protect devices and documents containing personal data against theft and loss.
Technical
We limit access to the people and permissions that are strictly needed, and encrypt communications.
Foreign environments
When personal data is handled on servers outside Japan, we take security measures based on an understanding of that country’s data protection rules.

7. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand and improve how our website is used. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as the pages you visit and how long you stay. This information does not include your name, email address, or other information that directly identifies you.

The information is handled in accordance with Google’s privacy policy. To learn how Google uses it, see “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.”

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, or stop measurement with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

8. Your requests

If you ask us to notify you of the purposes of use of, disclose, correct, add to, delete, stop using, erase, or stop providing to third parties your retained personal data, we will verify your identity and respond in accordance with the law.

9. Contact

For questions about how we handle personal information, please use our contact form.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as needed. When we do, we will post the changes on this page.
