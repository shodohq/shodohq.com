Information security policy

As a company that builds security products, Shodo Inc. treats protecting the information our customers entrust to us, and our own information assets, as a core management responsibility. We are committed to the following.

  1. 1LeadershipOur leadership drives a continuous commitment to information security.
  2. 2OrganizationWe appoint a person responsible for information security and maintain a management structure.
  3. 3Protecting entrusted informationWe use information our customers entrust to us only for the agreed purposes, with the minimum permissions needed.
  4. 4ComplianceWe comply with laws, standards, and contractual obligations related to information security.
  5. 5Incident responseIf an incident occurs, we investigate promptly, minimize the damage, and work to prevent it from happening again.
  6. 6Continuous improvementWe review our practices regularly and keep improving them.

Reporting vulnerabilities

We welcome reports from anyone who finds a vulnerability in our website or products. We treat every report with care, to protect the people who use our services.

Where to report

security@shodohq.com You can also choose “Vulnerability report” in our contact form.

Scope

Our website and the products and services we provide.

What to include

  • The affected URL or product
  • Steps to reproduce
  • The expected impact
  • How to contact you

When testing, please

  • Do not access, modify, or delete other users’ data.
  • Avoid testing that disrupts our services, such as sending large volumes of requests.
  • Do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until it has been fixed.

What we will do

  1. 1Confirm receipt within three business days.
  2. 2Investigate and let you know what we found and how we plan to address it.
  3. 3Tell you when the fix is complete.
